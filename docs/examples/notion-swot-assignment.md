## Your task

Notion, a productivity and collaboration platform, has hired you as a consultant to
evaluate its competitive position and identify opportunities for growth.

Conduct an **independent SWOT analysis of Notion** and use your research to develop a
strategic recommendation.

## 1. Research the company

- Explore Notion's website to understand its products, target customers, and value proposition.
- Find **at least two additional credible sources** that help you understand the company, its competitors, or the productivity software industry.

## 2. Build your SWOT analysis

Identify **one finding for each category**:

- **Strength:** What internal advantage gives Notion a competitive edge?
- **Weakness:** What internal limitation could hinder Notion's success?
- **Opportunity:** What external trend or market development could benefit Notion?
- **Threat:** What external factor could negatively affect Notion's growth or competitive position?

For **each finding**, provide:

1. A brief explanation (**1–2 sentences**).
2. Supporting evidence from your research.
3. A source link.

## 3. Recommend a strategic initiative

What is **one strategic initiative** Notion should prioritize over the next **1–3 years**?

In **100–150 words**, explain:

- What you're recommending.
- Which findings from your SWOT support your recommendation.
- One potential challenge Notion might face in implementing it.
